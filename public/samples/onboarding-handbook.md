# Northwind Ops Onboarding Handbook

*Fictional sample document for the Groundwork demo. Northwind Ops is not a real company.*

Welcome to Northwind Ops. This handbook covers your first weeks: what happens on day one, who helps you, and what to finish by when.

## Your first day

- Arrive at 9:30 a.m. at your assigned office. Reception will have your badge ready.
- IT hands you your laptop at 10:00 a.m. and walks you through setting up the VPN and your password manager.
- Lunch is with your team. Your manager picks the place, and the company pays.
- At 2:00 p.m. you join the weekly new-hire welcome session with People Operations.

## Your onboarding buddy

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from a different team. Your buddy meets you for 30 minutes twice a week during your first month. Ask them anything: where things are, who owns what, or how decisions get made.

## Your first 90 days

| By | Goal |
|---|---|
| Day 30 | Finish security training and benefits enrollment. Meet everyone on your team one to one. |
| Day 60 | Own one small project end to end and present it at your team meeting. |
| Day 90 | Agree on your goals for the next two quarters with your manager. |

You work from your assigned office for your first 90 days. After that, see the Remote Work Policy for remote days.

## Benefits and time off

- Enroll in health benefits within 30 days of your start date. If you miss the window, you wait until the next open enrollment in November.
- Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per year, plus company holidays. Request time off in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days.
- Sick days are separate from paid time off and do not need to be requested in advance.
